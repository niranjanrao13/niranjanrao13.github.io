About

I work at Target, a Fortune 50 retail giant, and have been part of several high-impact projects over the course of the past 3 years. I designed and built an optimization engine to reduce purchase order cost. This gave me a great introduction to the exciting space of order fulfillment, taught me operations research, and much more.
I trained a graph neural network model to develop embeddings for Target guests - powering a plethora of applications from nearest neighbour search to clustering.
In my current role, I have built a multi-agent system to run simulations for perishable item orders at Target stores - saving upwards $15M of savings by reducing wastage and improving utilization.
